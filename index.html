Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Bot: NayZ · Operator: KirbZy

This policy explains what NayZ processes and why.

Information the bot processes

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, channel and role IDs are processed to run commands, assign roles, send configured messages and record moderation actions. Usernames and server names may appear in responses or logs.
  • Server configuration and warnings: saved in guild_config.json on the host machine: prefix, welcome/leave templates and channels, moderation-log channel, event-log channel and types, auto-role, ticket settings (category, support role, panel, open-ticket IDs/status), AutoMod settings (keywords, filters, alert channel, timeout length, exemptions), anti-raid settings (enabled, threshold, response, alert channel), and warnings (user ID, moderator ID, reason, time; up to 50 per member).
  • Anti-raid: if enabled by an admin, non-bot joins are tracked in memory over a 10-second window. At the threshold the bot posts an alert and can optionally timeout burst joiners for 10 minutes, including later joiners during a 60-second response period. This data is memory-only and cleared on restart. Timeouts require the right Discord permission and role position.
  • Support tickets: opening a ticket creates a private channel visible to the member, the support role and the bot. On close, the bot compiles a text transcript (timestamps, usernames and IDs, message text, attachment links, embed titles/descriptions) and DMs it to the opener. If the DM fails, the ticket stays open so it can be retried. After success the channel is deleted; if deletion fails it is marked closed and hidden when possible. Transcripts are not saved by the bot and are truncated above 7 MB. Discord may retain messages or DMs under its own policies.
  • AutoMod: admins can create Discord-native rules (custom keywords, word-list presets, spam, harmful links, mention spam, display-name keywords) with block actions, alerts, optional timeouts and exemptions. Rules live in Discord, which processes messages and profile changes to enforce them. See Discord's AutoMod FAQ.
  • Server-event and audit logs: admins can enable join/leave, message edit/delete, channel and role create/delete, voice change and audit-log events, sent to a chosen channel and not saved locally. Audit entries may include actor, action, target and reason. Message logs contain author and channel metadata only, not message text. Audit entries need the View Audit Log permission.
  • Moderation actions: actions are carried out through Discord; if a moderation-log channel is set, it receives the action, affected user ID, moderator ID and reason.
  • Reports and suggestions: reports go to the moderation-log channel with the reporter's ID and details. Slash reports are private; prefix reports are deleted (needs Manage Messages). Suggestions are posted publicly with the submitter's name.
  • Aggregate analytics: counts of non-bot messages, joins/leaves and moderation actions per server per UTC day, kept in analytics.json for up to 30 days. No message text or per-user activity is stored.
  • Reminders: user ID, channel and server IDs, text and due time are saved in reminders.json and delivered by DM. If DMs are closed, a generic notice (without the text) may be posted in the original channel. The record is removed after delivery; up to 20 pending per user.
  • AI prompts: +ask sends your question to Google Gemini. If you opt in with +start, your DM messages and recent context go to Gemini. Up to 12 recent entries per opted-in user are kept in memory only. +forget clears context, +stop opts out and clears it, and a restart clears everything. Server messages are not used for free-form AI chat.
  • Discord messages: commands and replies pass through Discord and may remain there under its policies.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to operate the features above. AI prompts and context are sent to Google through the Gemini API; Google's handling depends on the applicable terms, account and region. Under current terms, unpaid-service content may be used to improve Google products and reviewed by people in some regions, with different handling for the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Don't submit sensitive information to AI commands. See Gemini API Additional Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Discord processes interactions under Discord's Privacy Policy, and the hosting provider may process technical data under its own policies. The operator does not use stored configuration or in-memory chat context for advertising, and cannot control copies kept by Discord, Google or the host.

Retention and deletion

Configuration and warnings stay in guild_config.json until the operator removes them or the configuration is deleted. Open-ticket records are removed after the channel is deleted, and closed ticket channels are deleted after the transcript DM. Analytics last up to 30 days. Reminders are removed after delivery. AI opt-in and context are memory-only and cleared by +stop or restart.

To ask about or request deletion of server data, email nayz.bot.support@gmail.com with the server ID and the information involved. Never send passwords, bot tokens or API keys. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and necessary security or operational records.

Security and children

Credentials are kept private and access to the host is restricted, but no internet service can promise perfect security. NayZ is not designed for children, and users must meet the age and availability requirements of Discord and Google's Gemini API.
